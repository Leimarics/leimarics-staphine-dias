About Me

The Voice Behind
Every Memorable Event

Hi, I'm Staphine — a professional Anchor & Emcee based in the heart of Goa, bringing warmth, energy, and elegance to events across India and beyond.

With over 7 years of experience and 800+ happy clients, I specialize in making every event feel uniquely curated — from intimate sangeet nights to large-scale corporate conferences and high-energy social events.

"The best anchor isn't just someone who talks — it's someone who makes every person in the room feel like they belong."

Fluent in English, Hindi, and Konkani, I adapt seamlessly to any audience, culture, and event format. Goa's vibrant spirit is in my DNA, and I bring that infectious energy to every stage I step on.
